01 · Getting Started

Sign In

Nook uses passwordless authentication. Enter your email, get a secure link — no passwords to remember, ever.

1
Open the app and you'll see the NOOK. sign-in screen. Type your email address into the input field.
2
Tap Continue. Nook instantly sends a secure magic link to your inbox.
3
Open your email and tap the link — it automatically opens Nook and signs you in. Done.
4
Prefer typing? Tap "Enter code instead" on the confirmation screen. Navigate to the OTP page and type your 6-digit code — Nook auto-submits the moment you type the last digit.

03 · Pairing Up

Creating & Joining a Space

A Space is the private, shared world you and your partner inhabit. One of you creates it; the other joins with a 6-character code.

🏡 Creating a Space

1
Tap Create a Space from the Onboarding Hub.
2
Nook instantly creates your space and generates a unique 6-character invite code.
3
Tap Copy code — or the share icon — to send the code to your partner via iMessage, WhatsApp, or any other app.
4
Once they join, tap "Continue to my space" to enter your feed.
Invite Code
A3KR7X

🔑 Joining a Space

1
Tap Join a Space from the Onboarding Hub.
2
Enter the 6-character code your partner shared (not case-sensitive).
3
Tap Join Space. Nook verifies the code and securely adds you. You'll land straight in the Home Feed.
🌿

Multiple Spaces: Nook supports more than one space. Tap your name on the Home Feed to open the Space Switcher and jump between them.

🔒

Security: Invite codes are unique and non-guessable. You can always find and re-share yours from the Space Profile tab.

05 · Daily Tab

Daily Prompts

Every day a fresh question arrives to help you and your partner discover more about each other.

🔒
The Dual-Unlock Mechanism
Keeps it honest

You cannot read your partner's answer until you've written your own. This prevents anchoring bias and leads to more genuine, independent responses. Once both partners answer, both responses reveal simultaneously.

How It Works

1
Tap the Daily tab (second tab in the bottom bar).
2
You'll see today's main question in a Sage-tinted Prompt Card at the top.
3
Your partner's response slot is blurred & locked until you submit your own.
4
Type your answer in the text field at the bottom and tap Send.
5
Once both partners have answered, both responses reveal on the card.
6
A live countdown shows how long until tomorrow's new prompt arrives.

Nudge your partner! If they haven't answered yet, tap the Nudge button to send them a push notification. There's a 2-hour cooldown to avoid spam.

✦ Custom & Additional Prompts

➕
Ask a Custom Question

Tap "Ask a Question" at the bottom of the Daily screen. Type any question and submit. It appears as an Additional Prompt card below the main one for both of you to answer anytime today.

✏️
Edit Your Response

Tap Edit on your submitted response to update your answer (same-day only). If you authored a custom question, you can also Delete it — removing the question and all responses after a confirmation.

📡

Works Offline: Nook caches 80+ prompts locally. Today's question generates even with no internet. Your response syncs automatically when you're back online.

08 · Security

App Lock

Require biometric authentication or your device PIN every time you open the app — keeping your private memories safe.

Enabling App Lock

1
Open the Space tab (fourth tab in the bottom bar).
2
Find the App Lock row with the lock icon.
3
Toggle the switch. The switch turns orange when App Lock is active.
🛡️
When It Locks

A biometric prompt appears on:

• Cold start — every time you open the app
• Background return — after 30+ seconds away from the app

Nook uses your device's Face ID, Touch ID, or PIN. Your biometric data is never stored by Nook.

🔑

Disabling: Simply toggle the switch back off in the Space tab. The preference persists between app restarts.

❓ Common Questions

Can I be in more than one Space?

Yes. Nook supports multiple spaces. Create or join additional spaces any time. Switch between them using the Space Switcher — tap your name on the Home Feed.

What happens if I leave a Space?

Your account is removed from that space. Your partner and all shared notes & memories remain intact — only you lose access. You can join or create another space immediately.

Can I edit a prompt response?

Yes — as long as it's still today's prompt. Tap Edit on your submitted response in the Daily tab to update your answer.

What does "Dual-Unlock" mean?

You must submit your own answer before you can see your partner's. This prevents anchoring bias — reading their answer first — and leads to more honest, independent responses. Once both are in, both reveal at the same time.
